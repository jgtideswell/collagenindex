How We Evaluate Collagen

The wellness market is saturated with bold claims, confusing jargon, and hidden dosages. We created the Collagen Index to cut through the marketing noise and provide a completely unbiased, easily searchable database. Our goal is simple: to help you compare the top collagen supplements side-by-side using standardised, transparent data.

Our Real-World Testing Process

We do not just scrape the internet for manufacturer claims or take marketing brochures at face value. We actively buy, consume, and test a vast majority of the products listed in our directory.

To ensure our reviews aren't skewed by one person's biology or taste preferences, we enlisted a wide network of friends, family, and colleagues to put these supplements to the test. By distributing these products across different age groups, lifestyles, and skin types, we gathered a broad, realistic spread of feedback. When you read the "Our Take" section on any product, you are reading the direct synthesis of this hands-on testing.

During our testing phase, our panel evaluates products based on three core criteria:

  • Solubility & Mixability: Does the powder dissolve seamlessly into hot coffee, or does it leave a gritty residue at the bottom of the mug? Does it clump in cold smoothies?
  • Taste & Palatability: Unflavoured should mean unflavoured. We flag products that carry a lingering fishy scent or a heavy, metallic aftertaste. For flavoured liquids and gummies, we evaluate whether they taste like a treat or a chore.
  • Noticeable Efficacy: While supplements take time to work, our testers actively monitor for early signs of success, including reduced morning joint stiffness, faster nail growth, less hair shedding, and improved skin hydration.

Standardising the Data

One of the biggest tricks in the supplement industry is hiding the actual collagen dosage behind proprietary blends or confusing serving sizes. A product might look cheap, but require you to take six large capsules a day just to hit a basic dose.

We do the math for you. Regardless of whether a product is a powder, a liquid sachet, or a gummy, we dig into the nutritional label and extract the exact amount of collagen per serving, listed in clear grams (g) or milligrams (mg). This allows you to sort our database from "High to Low" and instantly see which products deliver the most potent dose.
