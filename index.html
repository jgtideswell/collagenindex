Marine Collagen vs. Bovine Collagen

One of the most frequent questions buyers have is choosing between marine and bovine sources. Marine collagen (sourced from fish skin and scales) is highly bioavailable and primarily consists of Type I collagen, making it the preferred choice for skin rejuvenation, hair vitality, and nail strength. Bovine collagen (sourced from pasture-raised or grass-fed cattle) provides a robust blend of Type I and Type III collagen, which is highly beneficial for muscle recovery, joint support, and gut health.

Liquid, Powder, or Capsules: Which is Best?

Collagen supplements come in a variety of formats to suit different lifestyles. Hydrolyzed collagen powder remains incredibly popular due to its versatility—brands like Planet Paleo and Edible Health offer unflavoured peptides that dissolve easily into your morning coffee or smoothies. For those seeking maximum convenience and high absorption rates, liquid collagen sachets (such as those from Absolute Collagen and Revive Collagen) provide pre-measured, potent doses ready to drink on the go.
