How to Calculate True Cost Per 10g Dose: Avoiding Packaging Illusions

Exposing Why a "£12 Bottle" Can Cost Five Times More Than a "£25 Tub"

Retail shelf prices are wildly misleading in the collagen category. A £10 bottle of capsules looks cheap next to a £25 tub of powder—until you calculate what you are actually paying for 10 grams of active collagen protein.

Key Takeaways

  • Formula: Total Active Yield (g) = Serving Dose (g) × Number of Servings.
  • Cost per 10g Dose = (Retail Price ÷ Total Active Yield) × 10.
  • A 500g bovine tub at £22 costs just £0.44 per 10g clinical dose; a £12 jar of 1g gummies (30g total) costs £4.00 per 10g—nearly 10x more expensive!
  • Always compare supplements by Total Active Protein Yield rather than sticker price.
